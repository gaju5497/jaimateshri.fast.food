Our Story

Welcome to Jai Mateshri Bhel & Fast Food

Since 1999, Jai Mateshri Bhel & Fast Food has been serving delicious, fresh, and hygienic street food to our customers.

What started with a passion for serving tasty and affordable food has grown into a trusted destination for food lovers in Udgir.

From refreshing Pani Puri and flavourful Bhel to delicious Dabeli, Pav Bhaji, Chinese dishes, Sandwiches, and more, we prepare every dish with care and attention to quality.
